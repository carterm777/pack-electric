# Design direction — Pack Electric

Assigned so that no two of the 25 sites in this campaign converge on the same
look. These are **starting positions with authority**, not idle suggestions: build
from them. You may deviate from any one of them, but the deviation costs you one
line of written justification in `SELECTION-LOG.md` explaining what the assigned
option lost against and why — the same discipline `section-style-repo.md` asks
for everywhere else.

**Primary style family:** editorial + textured-organic
**Page ambition level:** mixed — a quiet page with two loud moments

## The specific risk on this brief

Fraunces on a warm cream ground with a rust accent is one keystroke away from the AI-default cream/serif/terracotta look front-end-design.md names. Break it deliberately: set Fraunces at a low optical size with tight tracking, lean on the deep teal as the dominant field rather than the cream, and keep the rust for small marks only.

## Signature element — the photo-diagnosis widget

Card-Based Widget Container pushed editorial — the widget reads as a mailed-in intake form, ruled and numbered

This is the most designed thing on the page. If a stranger screenshotted one
element from this site, it should be this one.

## Carry these through the page

- **Image technique to feature:** Overlapping or Bleeding Image — from `references/image-and-visual-richness.md`. Use it at least twice, deliberately.
- **Motion technique to feature:** Clip Reveal on every section title — from `references/animation-and-motion-richness.md`.

Both are on top of, not instead of, the per-section choices above. Read the real
entry in the reference file before implementing either — the Avoid-when field
applies to you.
